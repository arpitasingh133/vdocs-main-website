Privacy Policy

At VDocs, we treat clinical data with the highest level of security and confidentiality. Our policies are compliant with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We collect only the data necessary to provide clinical summarization services. This includes:

  • Clinical Documents: PDF reports, scanned images, and test results uploaded by the healthcare provider.
  • User Information: Name, contact details, and professional credentials of the doctors/clinics using our service.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you access and use the platform (e.g., timestamps, features used).

All clinical data is processed ephemerally where possible and stored securely only for the duration required by the healthcare provider's workflow.

2. ABDM & HIPAA Compliance

VDocs adheres to the rigorous standards set by:

  • ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission): Implementation of Health IDs (ABHA) and secure Health Information Exchange (HIE) protocols.
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or clinical data. Data is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent for interoperability purposes (e.g., sharing a summary with another specialist).
  • To comply with legal obligations or court orders.
  • With trusted service providers (e.g., cloud infrastructure) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.
